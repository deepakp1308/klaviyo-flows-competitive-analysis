01What “Flows” is

Flows are Klaviyo's automated, multi-step customer journeys—what other vendors call "automations," "drip campaigns," or "journeys." Each flow is started by a trigger (behavior, list/segment join, event, date, or webhook), then executes a sequence of messages, delays, conditional splits, trigger splits, A/B tests, and webhooks across channels.

Klaviyo positions Flows as the activation layer of its B2C CRM: every message is informed by a unified real-time customer profile (orders, browse, predictive scores, service tickets) rather than a static list. That data-first posture is the through-line of Klaviyo's pitch since 2012 and the basis of its differentiation vs. legacy ESPs.

  • 60+ pre-built flows: welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, price drop, back-in-stock, replenishment
  • Flows AI: describe the flow in plain English; Klaviyo builds triggers, splits, and structure

  • Library is segmented by industry vertical and lifecycle stage

04AI inside Flows — the K:AI story

Klaviyo splits AI into predictive, assistive, and autonomous. Flows now sit at the intersection of all three:

  • Flows AI (assistive): natural-language flow generation incl. trigger, splits, delays, and structure. Available on paid accounts with 250+ active profiles.
  • Marketing Agent (autonomous): learns the brand from its website, generates on-brand campaigns, and automatically sets must-have flows live; "optimize growth" loop tests timing/creative/offers and applies winners (rolling out).
  • Personalized Send Time / Smart Send Time: per-profile optimal delivery time across channels.
  • Channel Affinity: picks email vs. SMS vs. push per profile.
  • Predicted CLV, churn risk, next-order date become flow filters and split conditions.
  • Image Remix (in-editor generative image edits) and AI subject line generator reduce content time inside flow steps.
  • MCP server: Klaviyo exposes a remote MCP so external agents (Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT) can read/act on Klaviyo data — a meaningful "open by design" bet.

10Strategic read — where they're strong

  • Data + activation in one product. The B2C CRM positioning means Flows aren't a tactic, they're the output of the platform.
  • Speed-to-value. 3-click Marketing Agent + 60+ templates + Flows AI dramatically compress the gap between "signed up" and "first revenue from automation."
  • Agentic direction is real. Marketing Agent and Customer Agent move Klaviyo from "tool" to "teammate." MCP exposure makes them ecosystem-friendly to Cursor/Claude/ChatGPT.
  • PLG flywheel. Free tier ships full flow capability — friction to expansion is just contact growth.
  • Shopify gravity. Deepest ecom integration plus largest DTC reference list (143K+ brands) makes them the default option in the buyer's consideration set.

11Where they're exposed (our opportunities)

  • B2B / non-ecom workflows remain a weak spot — playbooks, lead routing, sales handoff, account-based journeys are not their native muscle.
  • Pricing complaints at scale. Public reviews cite confusing tier upgrades, billing for inactive profiles, and support quality friction at higher tiers — opening for transparent, predictable pricing.
  • Bring-your-own-AI / model choice. K:AI is opinionated; teams that want to plug their own models or fine-tune have only the MCP escape hatch.
  • Service depth at enterprise. Bloomreach-class omnichannel + governance is still considered more "enterprise-grade"; Klaviyo is closing this with Helpdesk, Customer Hub, and Customer Agent — but not closed yet.
  • Marketer trust in autonomous AI. "Marketing Agent sets your must-have flows live" is bold — many brands will want preview/approval guardrails before they cede that ground.
