Stride Athletics — Synthetic Demo Pack
========================================

Fictional direct-to-consumer athletic brand for prototyping email, SMS, and WhatsApp
campaigns. No affiliation with any real retailer.

Contents
--------
  images/          20 royalty-bounded JPEGs (Unsplash License). See ATTRIBUTION.txt.
  manifest.json    Product-style records (SKU, copy, price, image paths) for tooling.
  campaigns/       Example channel copy (plain text) you can paste into a composer.

Paths are relative to this folder. From the Phase 1 prototype, image URLs look like:
  synthetic-demo-pack/images/01-modern-gym-led.jpg
