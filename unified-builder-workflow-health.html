Key Drop-Off Insights:
  • Free customers have the lowest activation rate (48.2%) and the highest Unexplored rate (23.2%). Nearly 1 in 4 Free users never enter the builder at all, and 31.4% abandon after entering. This is where Brand Kit auto-extract, AI Setup Agent, and industry templates have the highest impact.
  • Paid < $299 customers lose 33% to Abandon (923K users/mo). This is the largest absolute abandonment pool and where template usability, content block bugs, and rendering issues directly erode paid retention.
  • Paid ≥ $299 customers achieve 82.2% activation with only 14.9% Abandon. Premium customers have the resources and motivation to push through friction, but their bugs are the highest-MRR-per-ticket.
  • Ecommerce has higher Abandon (35.2%) than Non-Ecommerce (31.3%) despite lower Unexplored rates. Ecommerce users enter the builder more often but get stuck more often, likely because templates don't match their lifecycle needs (abandoned cart, post-purchase, etc.).
  • Non-Ecommerce has higher Unexplored (7.8% vs 5.3%). Professional services customers are less likely to even start, reinforcing the B2B/ProServ template gap identified in the roadmap.
