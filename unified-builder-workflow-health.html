Trend: Are We Getting Better or Worse?

Comparing negative VOC volume per workflow stage: Jul–Oct 2025 (baseline) vs Feb–May 2026 (current). Source: unitQ. Improving = >15% decrease. Flat = ±15%. Worsening = >15% increase.

Workflow Stage Jul–Oct 2025 Feb–May 2026 Change Trend What This Means
1. Choose Structure 795 569 −28.4% Improving Template navigation and campaign creation complaints are declining. Prior investments in builder UX are paying off.
2. Establish Brand & Style 52 106 +103.8% Worsening Brand Kit complaints more than doubled. Font upload failures, logo preview bugs, and styles reverting across assets are accelerating. The partially-ramped Brand Kit incident fix is not resolving the problem. Highest-priority regression.
3. Add & Place Content 96 81 −15.6% Improving Drag-and-drop and content block issues are marginally improving. Still a high-volume pain point (157 items in current period with broader queries).
4. Refine & Polish 149 131 −12.1% Flat Text formatting, copy/paste, and spacing issues are not improving meaningfully. Consistent pain that isn't getting addressed.
5. Personalize & Tailor 121 99 −18.2% Improving Merge tags, segmentation, and personalization issues declining. Still the highest MRR exposure stage ($38.3K/mo) due to high-value customer concentration.
6. Preview & Validate 278 159 −42.8% Improving Largest improvement across all stages. Preview and rendering complaints nearly halved. Still the highest MRR exposure ($60.7K/mo) but trajectory is positive.
7. Learn + Reuse 14 12 −14.3% Flat Low volume, not moving. Universal Content and save-as-template capabilities haven't shipped yet, so no improvement expected until they do.
Action Required: Establish Brand & Style is the only worsening stage (+104%). Brand Kit font uploads, logo previews, and style propagation are regressing while every other stage is improving or flat. This directly blocks the "AI That Knows Your Brand" tenet and the Brand compliance score initiative. The Q1 roadmap items (Brand Kit data correctness fix, font upload fix, Brand voice profile) are urgent.
