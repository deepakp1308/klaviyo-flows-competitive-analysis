Net Assessment vs Canva: Mailchimp leads or matches in 19 of 22 capabilities. Canva’s 3 leads are on its home turf: visual editor polish (13-year head start), template volume (3.6M+ vs purpose-built email), and multi-brand scale (1,000 kits). Every ESP-specific capability — deliverability, segmentation, analytics, automation, personalization, ecommerce, A/B testing, rendering fidelity — is a structural advantage Canva cannot replicate without becoming a full ESP. The competitive risk is not Canva building an ESP. It’s the “design in Canva, send through cheap ESP” workflow becoming normative among SMBs who don’t value the intelligence layer. Mailchimp’s defense is making the AI so good that leaving the platform means losing visible, measurable value.
