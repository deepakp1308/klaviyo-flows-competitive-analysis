Appendix C: Assumptions & Caveats

  • Modeled estimates, not actuals. All revenue numbers are forward-looking modeled estimates based on cohort sizing, lift assumptions, and causal share discounting. They are not measured actuals.
  • Causal shares are conservative. Editor causal shares (20–50%) intentionally discount contribution to avoid over-claiming. Actual impact may be higher or lower depending on feature adoption curves.
  • No compounding. Each initiative's revenue is calculated independently. We do not compound benefits (e.g., an adoption lift that also improves repeat is counted in the higher-impact lever only).
  • Multi-lever contributions. Many initiatives contribute to multiple revenue channels simultaneously (e.g., Brand Kit Auto-Extract appears in Upgrades, Free-to-Paid, and Trial-to-Paid). The per-channel amounts reflect the cohort-specific impact.
  • Within-lever splits are proportional estimates. Per-initiative dollar splits within a given lever (e.g., the breakdown of PA4's $3.1M across 10 initiatives) are proportional estimates based on relative impact assessment, not independently measured.
  • Attribution requires controlled experimentation. To validate these estimates, each initiative needs controlled A/B testing with holdout groups. These numbers should be treated as planning inputs, not commitments.
  • ARPU values are blended. At-booking ARPU ($45.35/mo) applies to new conversions; steady-state ARPU ($91.44/mo) applies to retention and upgrade calculations. Actual per-plan ARPU varies.
  • Time horizon is annual. All figures are annualized ARR impact assuming full rollout. Actual ramp depends on ship dates and adoption curves.
